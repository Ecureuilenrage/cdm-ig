CAPTION
13 goals. One tournament. Borrowed boots.

Just Fontaine's 1958 run is the kind of record that stops being a stat and becomes a legend:

● 13 goals in six games — still the most ever scored in a single World Cup
● The closest since: Gerd Müller, 10 in 1970. Nobody else within three.
● And he did it in boots borrowed from a teammate after his own pair broke.

Numa has filed this one under "untouchable".
